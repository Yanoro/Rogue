The tile is old flagstone with moss in the joints, so I want a slab that has been
walked on for a long time: cool grey stone, narrow dark joints, and moss only where
water would sit.

```sprite
sprite 32x32
kind tile
material stone
fill stone.2
line (0,12) (31,12) color=stone.0
line (0,24) (31,24) color=stone.0
line (11,0) (11,12) color=stone.0
line (22,12) (22,24) color=stone.0
fill_area (5,4) color=stone.3
fill_area (16,4) color=stone.4
fill_area (5,18) color=stone.4
scatter color=stone.1,stone.3 density=0.12 seed=21 cluster=2
pixel (11,11) color=grass.0
pixel (12,11) color=grass.1
```
